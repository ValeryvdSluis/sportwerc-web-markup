Why SportWerc?

Sports clubs depend on volunteers to keep their activities running. However, finding a referee, bar volunteer or coach can sometimes be difficult.

SportWerc was created to make it easier for clubs to find help and for volunteers to find opportunities to contribute.

Who is SportWerc for?

Sports clubs

Clubs can post tasks when they need someone to help with an activity, match or event.

Volunteers

People who want to help can browse available tasks and choose something that fits their availability.

Types of tasks

Tasks you can find on SportWerc

  • Refereeing
  • Bar duty
  • Coaching
  • Event support
  • Other club activities
