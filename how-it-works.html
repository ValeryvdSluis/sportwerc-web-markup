Four simple steps

  1. A sports club posts a task, for example a referee for a match.
  2. The task appears in the list of available tasks.
  3. A volunteer finds a task that fits their availability.
  4. The volunteer and the club contact each other and agree on the details.

Who does what?

For sports clubs

Fill in the Post a task form with the date, time, location and a short description. Add an email address so volunteers can reach you.

For volunteers

Browse the available tasks, choose a task that suits you and contact the club. You decide when and where you want to help.
